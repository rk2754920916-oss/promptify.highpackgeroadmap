20+ LPA ke liye 4 pillars

1. DSA + Problem Solving

Product companies ka pehla filter. LeetCode, Codeforces ya GFG par roz 1-2 problems. Target: 400+ solved, 5-6 contests per year.

2. Core CS

OS, DBMS, CN, OOPs aur System Design basics. Interview mein yahi poochha jata hai.

3. Projects + Internship

Deployed projects with real users. Ek internship minimum, do ho to behtar. Resume ka sabse strong hissa.

4. Profile + Network

GitHub, LinkedIn, resume, referrals. Open source ya hackathon se naam banta hai.

Kaun si companies 20+ LPA deti hain

Product-based

  • Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Adobe
  • Atlassian, Uber, Flipkart, Walmart
  • PhonePe, Razorpay, CRED, Zomato

Quant / Trading

  • Tower, Jane Street, Optiver
  • Graviton, Quadeye
  • Strong math + DSA chahiye

Off-campus route

  • Referral + resume + OA
  • Startups (funded) with ESOPs
  • Internship se PPO

Note: Syllabus university ke hisaab se thoda alag ho sakta hai (WBUT/MAKAUT, AKTU, etc.). Yeh roadmap skills par based hai, subject names par nahi. Package kisi ko guarantee nahi hota; consistency, projects aur interview prep se chances kaafi badhte hain.
